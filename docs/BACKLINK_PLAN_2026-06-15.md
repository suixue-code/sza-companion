## Decision

Yes, we need backlinks, but not broad paid link building.

The reason is specific:

- Similarweb shows `survivezombiearena.com` gets 12,026 May 2026 visits and 85.68% of its traffic from organic search.
- The `survive zombie arena wiki` query is winnable by topical domains: `survivezombiearena.com` has 34.39% share, `survivezombiearena.wiki` has 14.05%, and several exact/near-match wiki domains appear.
- The `survive zombie arena codes` head term is dominated by large sites and YouTube: Beebom, Pocket Tactics, IGN, YouTube, PCGamesN. Backlinks alone will not beat them quickly.
- Our best linkable assets are source receipts, tools, and update trackers. Those need citations and community references to look trustworthy.

## What Not To Do

- Do not buy generic "100 gaming backlinks" packages.
- Do not mass-submit spun guest posts.
- Do not post exploit/script content for links.
- Do not create a second thin wiki/domain just to self-link.
- Do not pitch `codes` alone to major sites until we have fresh in-game screenshots.
